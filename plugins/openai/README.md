# Protocol

Protocol helps service businesses get found and trusted. This plugin reviews
public website content and crawl signals so owners can choose practical
marketing improvements based on evidence.

Connect the hosted MCP service at **https://plugins.protocoltext.com/mcp**.
No account, API key or sign-in is required for this version. Supply public HTTPS
website URLs without query parameters, credentials, fragments or custom ports.

Try “Review https://protocoltext.com and suggest three useful improvements,”
“Check the crawl signals for https://ahxelabs.com,” or “Compare these two public
business websites using observable marketing signals.”

The tools inspect public HTML titles, descriptions, headings, contact/booking
signals, canonical URLs, robots directives, Open Graph metadata, JSON-LD types,
robots.txt and the conventional sitemap location. Comparisons cover two or
three public websites.

Results include the source URL, capture time and limitations. This is not a
rendered-browser audit, speed test, search-ranking measurement or proof of AI
visibility. It cannot access private analytics, modify websites, publish, book
appointments or send messages. JavaScript-only content may be absent. A
detected signal does not prove that it works or produces customers.

## Privacy and support

The application processes public URLs and fetched observations in memory. It
does not save them in a database, sell them or train models on them. Cloudflare
hosts the service and resolves public DNS; requested websites receive a fetch.
Your AI platform processes returned results under its own policies. Never
submit credentials, private customer data or private review links.
